SOUND ARMOURY – END USER LICENCE AGREEMENT

This document outlines permissible ways to use Sound Armoury sound libraries by owners of any product
licences.

This document applies to Sound Armoury sound libraries when purchased from stores without store-wide
EULAs, such as:
• Itch.io
This document does not apply to Sound Armoury sound libraries purchased from stores that do have
store-wide EULAs, such as:
• Asoundeffect
• Unity Asset Store

Permissible usage
The following (non-exhaustive) list presents permissible usage of Sound Armoury sound libraries for which
a valid licence has been purchased or otherwise legitimately obtained:
• Use within commercial or non-commercial projects is permissible, regardless of whether the sound has
been modified or not
o Permissible types of projects include any form of media, such as video games, films, podcasts,
etc.
• Sound Armoury sound libraries may be shared amongst working teams, but no team members may
use Sound Armoury sound libraries on other projects without themselves acquiring legitimate licences
for the sound libraries.
o For example, two people (“Person A” and “Person B”) are working on a game. Person A owns
a Sound Armoury sound library, and shares a sound library with Person B for them to use
together on this game. Person B is permitted to use the sound library as part of the project, but
Person B is not permitted to use the sound library as part of any project not involving Person A.

Non-permissible usage
The following (non-exhaustive) list presents non-permissible usage of Sound Armoury sound libraries:
• Training of generative AI
• Usage within political campaigning is not permitted, unless explicitly agreed by Sound Armoury
• Redistributing Sound Armoury content, whether for free or for a price, is not permitted without explicit
permission from Sound Armoury. This applies whether the content has been modified or not.
