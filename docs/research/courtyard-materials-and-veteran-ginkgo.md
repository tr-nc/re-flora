# Courtyard visual reference study

## Evidence and limits

- [Getty Art & Architecture Thesaurus: iron oxide red](https://www.getty.edu/vow/AATFullDisplay?find=300310162&logic=AND&note=&subjectid=300013537): search-index passage identifies hematite / iron-oxide earth reds. [Getty conservation spectroscopy](https://www.getty.edu/conservation/publications_resources/pdf_publications/pdf/infrared_spectroscopy.pdf) identifies red hematite as Fe₂O₃. Magnetite (Fe₃O₄) is black, not the red pigment being sought. These are pigment identities, **not calibrated wall RGB measurements**.
- [Palace Museum pigment investigation](https://www.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/File/2022/08/11/u62f4956979f9d.pdf): search-index evidence identifies iron-oxide earth red in a sampled decorative material. It does not establish that all palace walls use that pigment. The game should evoke a muted, earthy iron-red plaster, not claim to reproduce a measured Forbidden City paint formula.
- [Palace Museum: roof-tile kiln excavation](https://www.dpm.org.cn/learing_detail/225888.html): the roof system distinguishes pan tiles, cover tiles and eaves components. [Palace Museum wall-cap study](https://www.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/file/2025/02/13/1739432353eIvyvIZcw260.pdf) specifically distinguishes wall-cap mounting and mentions wall-specific cover-tile heads and drip components. Glazed imperial details are not evidence that an ordinary grey courtyard wall should be glazed or equally ornamented.
- [US National Park Service, Preservation Brief 30](https://www.nps.gov/orgs/1739/upload/preservation-brief-30-clay-tile-roofs.pdf): search-index passage describes concave pan tiles and convex cover tiles covering their joints. Direct PDF retrieval timed out in this session. Use this to understand pan-and-cover construction, not as an assertion that all Chinese roofs share one system.
- [RHS: Ginkgo biloba](https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/7990/ginkgo-biloba/details/): full extracted description distinguishes a conical young crown from an irregular, spreading mature crown. Fan-shaped leaves and yellow autumn foliage identify the species.
- [NC State Extension: Ginkgo biloba](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/ginkgo-biloba/): page read in a browser, including its photograph viewer. Jim Robbins' photograph labelled “Large, rounded tree with golden yellow leaves” shows overlapping, densely foliated upright branches forming an uneven, multi-peaked crown, not a straight pole with sparse terminal fans. Its “Trunk with rough, furrowed bark” photograph documents rough mature bark. Photograph licence is CC BY-NC-ND 4.0; references were viewed only and **are not imported or redistributed**.
- [State Forestry and Grassland Administration: Juxian ancient ginkgo](https://www.forestry.gov.cn/c/www/dzbhdt/635288.jhtml): primary institutional context for old ginkgo specimens. [Kew ginkgo](https://www.kew.org/plants/ginkgo-tree) search-index passages corroborate irregular spreading crowns; direct fetch was HTTP 403, so this is corroboration, not a full-page review.

## Art-direction inferences (not botanical simulation)

Muted warm iron red should retain brown/earth undertones under the actual game lighting. A numerical sRGB swatch is an authored choice, not a chemical property. Check it in the same sun/exposure as the current courtyard.

A readable miniature pan-and-cover cap needs a concave channel / convex roll rhythm along the wall, tile courses lapping downhill across its width, a raised ridge and articulated eaves. Keep a continuous mortar/roof bed beneath joints. At roughly 20 voxels across the default wall cap, these are stylized construction cues; sub-voxel engraving cannot substitute for silhouette and relief.

The courtyard specimen needs its own persistent veteran form: basal flare, a heavy irregular trunk, unequal ascending scaffold forks, many smaller leaf-bearing shoots and overlapping fan sprays. Keep gaps that reveal branches, but not large empty spaces over almost the whole crown. Ordinary/previously saved ginkgo morphology must remain reconstructible; new form metadata should default to the previous form when absent. Leaves must remain attached to real branches and use the ordinary growth, wind, publication and persistence path.

No external image, texture, model, audio or AI-generated asset is imported by this study. Native game screenshots are validation evidence, not botanical references.
